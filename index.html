1. Business Context & Critical Turnover Drivers Empirical Diagnostics

Enterprise analysis demonstrates that employee attrition is not an organizational-wide contagion, but a concentrated, surgical operational failure. Departures are heavily clustered within low-equity, early-career operational staff subject to aggressive overtime demands.

Primary Flight Catalysts
  • The Overtime Squeeze: Employees required to work overtime experience a 30.5% exit rate (127 leavers, representing 53.6% of all company exits), compared to just 10.4% among standard-hour staff.
  • Equity Ownership As An Anchor: Staff with zero stock options register 24.4% attrition (154 exits). Granting Level-1 equity participation collapses turnover to 8.5% (~65% reduction).
  • Role-Level Exposure: Sales Representatives face an extreme 39.8% attrition rate (33 leavers), followed by Laboratory Technicians at 23.9% (62 leavers). 84.0% of all company departures originate from just 4 operational roles.
Career & Demographic Outliers
  • The Year-1 Onboarding Cliff: 75 of 237 departures (31.6%) exit within their very first year of tenure, indicating an acute onboarding vulnerability in fast-paced production roles.
  • Tenure Stagnation Anomaly: Identification of 29 long-tenured veteran leavers (7–15 years without promotion), including Senior Research Directors with up to 31 years of tenure exiting due to career ceilings.
  • Toxic Compounding Hazard: Single employees working overtime with zero equity in entry roles experience turnover exceeding 50.0%.
2. VertiPaq Star Schema & Architectural Design Production Data Model

Rather than querying a fragile single flat table, the architecture establishes a normalized Star Schema inside Power BI VertiPaq. This separates high-volume transaction records from analytical dimensions, optimizing RAM compression and query performance.

Table Name Type Key Columns / Grain Cardinality
Fact_Employee Central Fact 1,470 records • PK: EmployeeNumber Core Grain
Dim_Department Dimension DeptID (PK), DepartmentName 1 : * (Single)

Core DAX Mathematical Engine
Attrition Rate % = DIVIDE(SUM(Fact_Employee[AttritionFlag]), COUNTROWS(Fact_Employee), 0) Overtime Attrition Gap = VAR OT = CALCULATE([Attrition Rate %], Fact_Employee[OverTime]="Yes") VAR NonOT = CALCULATE([Attrition Rate %], Fact_Employee[OverTime]="No") RETURN OT - NonOT
Architectural Note: Relationships use 1-to-many unidirectional filters. Active/inactive links for satisfaction metrics are switched dynamically via USERELATIONSHIP() to preserve star schema integrity.

3. Multi-Tool Analytics Synergy: SQL Engine & Python ETL Tasks 7 & 8 Completed

Demonstrating senior-level proficiency across the enterprise data stack: raw transactional data is extracted and aggregated via ANSI SQL, stratified and statistically tested in Python, and visualized interactively in Power BI.

Task 7: SQL Grouped Aggregation (SQL_Test.md)
SELECT d.DepartmentName AS Department, jr.JobRoleName AS JobRole, COUNT(CASE WHEN f.Attrition = 'Yes' THEN 1 END) AS AttritionCount, ROUND(AVG(f.MonthlyIncome), 2) AS AvgMonthlyIncome FROM Fact_Employee f JOIN Dim_Department d ON f.DeptID = d.DeptID JOIN Dim_JobRole jr ON f.RoleID = jr.RoleID GROUP BY d.DepartmentName, jr.JobRoleName ORDER BY AttritionCount DESC, AvgMonthlyIncome ASC;

Executes clean star-schema joins. Accurately identifies top leaver volumes in Lab Technicians (62 leavers, $3,237) and Sales Executives (57 leavers, $6,924).

5. Strategic Action Plan & Estimated Business ROI 3 Prioritized Interventions
Priority & Intervention Target Audience Operational Mechanism Projected Retention ROI
1. Workload Caps & Overtime Relief Sales Representatives & Lab Technicians Implement strict 10-hr/week operational overtime caps; rebalance sales territory quotas; hire ~8 floaters for peak intake. Reduces OT attrition from 30.5% to ~18%, preserving ~35 exits/year.
2. Equity & Stock Option Grants Level-0 Equity Staff in Core Production Roles Extend Level-1 equity participation to high-performing entry/mid operators; apply a 2-year vesting cliff to anchor tenure. Cuts zero-equity flight risk by ~65%; anchors 30+ key operators.
3. Onboarding & Promotion Pathways Year 1–3 Hires & Stagnant Veterans (≥4 yrs no promo) Structured 90-day onboarding buddy system; annual HR audits for stagnant staff; clear dual-track (specialist vs. manager) paths. Eliminates Year-1 cliff (31.6% of exits); curtails veteran brain drain.
Total Enterprise Financial Impact & Replacement Cost Savings

Lowering turnover by 4.0% (from 16.1% to 12.1%) prevents ~59 exits annually. At an industry benchmark replacement cost of 1.5× annual salary (~$75,000 per leaver), this yields $3.2M – $4.4M in direct cost savings, self-funding all recommended interventions.
